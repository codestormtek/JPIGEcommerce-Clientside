---
name: Kiosk interaction design
description: Usability constraints that must survive future visual redesigns of the customer kiosk.
---

The kiosk can adopt bold smokehouse styling and condensed display type, but transactional controls must remain fast, legible, and accessible: visible focus, at least 44px touch targets, reduced-motion behavior, and responsive landscape/portrait layouts.

**Why:** A visual redesign initially preserved the ordering state machine but unintentionally hid keyboard focus, reduced key touch targets, and added unconditional motion.

**How to apply:** Treat setup, menu, modifiers, cart, customer details, payment, cancellation, and confirmation as one critical journey. Keep display type out of body copy, preserve all real API/payment behavior, and review accessibility after every styling pass.
