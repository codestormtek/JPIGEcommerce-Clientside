---
name: Shared kiosk and pickup menu
description: Product policy for keeping online pickup an exact catalog mirror of the kiosk.
---

Online pickup must mirror the kiosk catalog, not maintain a separate product allowlist. Share category grouping and ordering rules while allowing layouts suited to each device.

**Why:** The user approved a single-source menu so changes made to the kiosk's catalog automatically appear online, without duplicate administration.

**How to apply:** Keep pickup hours and capacity controls independent, but do not introduce pickup-only catalog selections or exclusions. Refresh open menus without losing carts or disrupting payments, and retain server-side price and availability validation.
